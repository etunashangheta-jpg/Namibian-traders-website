How it works

Five steps, one platform

Everything between "I want to run this" and "here's my finishing time" happens in one place — for the runner and the organiser. Payment is required to complete a registration.

01

Register

Pick a race and distance, create a profile once and reuse it for every future entry.

02

Pay

Complete payment at checkout (card or mobile money). No payment = no confirmed entry.

03

Collect bib

Pick up a numbered bib with a QR code linked to your registration (QR check-in is planned).

04

Cross the line

Your watch records splits and finish times — results sync to the platform.

05

See your time

Official results post to the live board and your profile within minutes once synced.

Beyond registration

One platform, every stage of the event

The platform is built to grow beyond registration into a complete event management system — a single command-centre for creating events, collecting payments, running race day and producing post-event reports. Calendar notifications are a core feature: participants and organisers get reminders for what's next.

Calendar

Event Calendar

Upcoming, open, closed and completed events in one calendar view — with add-to-calendar and reminder actions for participants.

New

Calendar Notifications & Reminders

Participants can add events to their personal calendars and receive reminders for registration deadlines, payment deadlines, race-pack collection and race day.

New

Push Notifications

Important event updates, changes and announcements delivered straight to participants via push, SMS or WhatsApp.

New

Admin Calendar

Organisers see operational dates and deadlines across multiple events in one view and receive reminders for key operational tasks.

Where this is headed

Built in phases, not all at once

Namibian Traders already organises races. Rather than lean on a timing provider indefinitely, we're building that capability in-house, one working phase at a time.

01In progress

Entry & payment

Race-entry portal, athlete database, online payment, automatic confirmation and participant lists.

02Planned

Timing

Bib assignment, race-day QR-code check-in, and results upload and integration (watch syncs and uploads).

03Planned

Athlete app

Race history, personal results, rankings and virtual-race uploads with Strava-style syncing.

04Planned

Community

GPS training tracking, clubs, challenges, leaderboards and notifications.

The goal isn't to copy an existing platform feature for feature — it's to own the registration and timing layer for our own events, then open it to other organisers once it's proven.
